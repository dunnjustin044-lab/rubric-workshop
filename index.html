Let’s build a rubric

Start with what students should learn. Then make the evidence, criteria and levels clear enough that students and markers know what quality looks like.

✦ Clear expectations✦ Useful feedback✦ More consistent marking

Step 1 · End in mind

What should students know and be able to do?

Start from the intended learning. A strong rubric makes the target visible in the work students produce.

01 / 05
◎
Constructive alignment
Check that the rubric assesses the learning outcomes students had a chance to practise through instruction.
Step 2 · Identify criteria

What parts of the work matter?

Choose 3–6 observable dimensions that reflect the core attributes of the outcome. Each criterion should assess a distinct part of the work.

02 / 05
🎯Evaluation criteriaThe essential dimensions or learning targets being assessed.
🔎ObservableUse wording a student can find in the work and a marker can point to.
🧩DistinctAvoid scoring the same quality twice under different names.

Step 3 · Determine the scale

How many levels will help explain quality?

Choose a scale that is practical for this task. Label the levels so they communicate what performance looks like, not just whether it is “good” or “bad.”

03 / 05
You can rename each level and change its score.
↗
Keep the steps meaningful
Each level should represent a noticeable difference in the quality or completeness of the evidence.
Step 4 · Draft descriptors

What would each level look like?

Write parallel descriptions for each criterion. Describe evidence in the work so students can act on the feedback and markers can apply the rubric consistently.

04 / 05
💡Descriptor recipe: name the evidence + describe its quality or extent. Keep the same focus across levels.
✎
For example
Instead of “excellent synthesis,” describe what the student does: “Connects ideas across sources to build a clear line of reasoning.”
Step 5 · Review & refine

Does this rubric work as a whole?

Review the three pillars, check the alignment, then note what you would test on sample student work.
