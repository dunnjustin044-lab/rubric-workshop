Literature Review Rubric

Assessment Criteria
Review of previous research
[30 marks]
Does not meet the required standard
(0 – 49%)
  • Less than five credible sources
  • The ability to synthesize and logically and coherently and critically describe and apply previous literature is not evident
  • Literature is summarized but not integrated
  • No gap identified
Meets the required standard
(50–64%)
  • Minimum of five credible sources used
  • Application of literature to the student’s research topic is evident
  • A good attempt at integrating and synthesizing the literature
  • Coherent structure and organization
  • Lacks depth and/or logical flow
Partially exceeds the required standard
(65–74%)
  • The review has sufficient depth of critical analysis
  • The construction of a solid argument is evident
  • Independent thinking is demonstrated through synthesis and integration of literature
  • The research problem is well positioned in the literature and the gap is articulated
  • Argumentation flows logically and is substantiated with references.
Greatly exceeds the required standard
(75–100%)
In addition:
  • The review indicates a deep understanding of the purpose of the literature review
  • The topic or problem is situated within broader scholarly literature
  • Sources are relatively recent
  • The ability to synthesize and logically discuss and present previous literature is demonstrated throughout.
